Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

01 — Information We Collect

The Odds Algo collects limited, non-personal data to improve the Service:

  • Stock tickers searched — no personally identifiable information attached
  • Usage data collected via Google Analytics 4, including pages visited, session duration, and approximate location at city level
  • Standard browser data such as device type and browser version

We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it via a feedback form.

02 — How We Use Your Information

Data collected is used solely to:

  • Understand how users interact with the Service
  • Improve the accuracy and user experience of the algorithmic scoring model
  • Measure aggregate usage and traffic patterns

03 — Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymous usage data. Google Analytics uses cookies to track user interactions. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

For more information on how Google uses your data, visit: google.com/policies/privacy/partners

04 — Cookies

The Service uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Google Analytics session tracking
  • Remembering your lookup count — stored locally, not transmitted to our servers

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Some features of the Service may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

05 — Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — usage tracking
  • Financial Modeling Prep — market data
  • Finnhub — analyst consensus data
  • SEC EDGAR — insider transaction data
  • Supabase — anonymous prediction logging

None of these services receive personally identifiable information from your use of The Odds Algo.

06 — Data Retention

Anonymous usage data is retained for up to 24 months for analytical purposes. Prediction logs stored in our database contain only ticker symbols, scores, and timestamps — no user identifiers.

07 — Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

08 — Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
